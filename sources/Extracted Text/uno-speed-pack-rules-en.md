### Required base game

THESE CARDS ARE DESIGNED TO BE COMBINED WITH A CLASSIC UNO® CARD GAME AND CANNOT BE PLAYED WITH ON THEIR OWN.

### UNO® SPEED PACK IN A NUTSHELL

In a normal round of UNO® the Speed Level is “one,” meaning each player plays 1 card on their turn. But play a card with a “Speed Up” icon and the Speed Level goes up to “two,” meaning everyone must play 2 cards: the card they would normally play, plus an additional card. Every “Speed Up” icon you play increases how many cards players must play on their turn until someone can’t match the speed and crashes!

### SPEED PACK ICONS

Every card with a “Speed Up” icon you play increases the speed by one level. Starting with the next player, everyone must play an extra card on their turn for each icon played. At Speed Level two, each player must play 2 cards on their turn. 3 cards at Speed Level three, 4 at four, and so on. When playing multiple cards on your turn, each card must match the previous played card. Stay sharp because every “Speed Up” icon you play increases the speed for the next player. And the Wild “Double Speed Up” card increases the speed by 2 levels and you get to choose the color that continues play!

### ACTION CARDS

Unlike “Speed Up” icons, Action Cards (Draw 2, Skip, Reverse, etc.) only take effect if they are the last card played. If you play 3 cards on your turn and the first or second card is an Action Card, the next player does not have to do the action.

### WHAT IF YOU CAN’T PLAY?

If a player is unable to play the number of cards required, they play what they can and then draw cards equal to the current Speed Level. For example, if the current speed is 4 and you are only able to play 2 cards, you play those 2 cards and then draw 4 cards. The speed is then reset to 1 and the next player only has to play 1 card on their turn.

### FOR COLORBLIND PLAYERS

Special graphic symbols have been added to each card to help identify the color(s) on that card. This will allow players with ANY form of color blindness to easily play!

## Visual context and extraction notes — editorial

- Ages 7+. Requires a classic UNO deck.
- The printed “Speed Up Icon” is a right-pointing double-chevron shape. The Wild Double Speed Up has two such speed-up marks; its +2 speed-level effect is stated in the text. These are speed increases, not draw-penalty numbers.
- The figure caption “SPEED UP ICON” was moved out of the sentence it interrupted.
- The source distinguishes speed-up triggers from Action Card effects: only a final Action Card takes effect, while every played speed-up icon increases the speed for the next player.
