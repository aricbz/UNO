### Required base game

THESE CARDS ARE DESIGNED TO BE COMBINED WITH A CLASSIC UNO® CARD GAME AND CANNOT BE PLAYED WITH ON THEIR OWN.

### UNO® STACK PACK IN A NUTSHELL

Stacking in UNO? Oh yes we did. Each Stack Card acts like a Draw Card you can stack to make other players draw more and more cards. Play a Stack 1, Stack 2, or the dreaded Wild Stack Number card and soon you’ll be passing out penalties like popcorn.

### STACK PACK CARDS

### STACK 1

You may play this card:

- on a card of the matching color. The next player must draw 1 card and lose their turn.

- in response to a Draw Card (+2, +4) if your card matches the color of the Draw 2 Card, or the chosen color for the Wild Draw 4. The total penalty is passed to the next player. For example, if a player plays a Wild Draw 4 and calls “red,” you may play a red Stack 1 card which makes the next player have to draw 5 cards and lose their turn.

- in response to a Stack Card. The penalty increases by 1 and the next player must draw the total of the Stack Cards. For example, if you play a Stack 1 in response to a Stack 2, the next player must draw 3 cards and lose their turn.

### STACK 2

Just like Stack 1, only you force the next player to draw 2 cards.

### WILD STACK 3

Just like above, except this card is wild, meaning you may play this card:

- on any other card. You choose the color that continues play and the next player must draw 3 cards and lose their turn.

- in response to any Draw Card or Stack Card. You choose the color that continues play and the penalty increases by 3 and passes to the next player.

### WILD STACK NUMBER

Same as the Wild Stack 3, except:

- To determine how many cards the next player must draw, flip over the top card of the Draw Pile until you reveal a Number Card. The number on the card is how many cards the next player must draw! Put any cards you flipped over under the Discard Pile.

### FOR COLORBLIND PLAYERS

Special graphic symbols have been added to each card to help identify the color(s) on that card. This will allow players with ANY form of color blindness to easily play!
