### Required base game

THESE CARDS ARE DESIGNED TO BE COMBINED WITH A CLASSIC UNO® CARD GAME AND CANNOT BE PLAYED WITH ON THEIR OWN.

### UNO® REVERSE PACK IN A NUTSHELL

This pack is loaded with so many Reverses it will make your head spin. Reverse Skip, Reverse Draw 2, Power Reverse?! So stay alert, because after you add these cards to your UNO® deck you’ll be wondering whose turn is it anyway?

### REVERSE PACK CARDS

### REVERSE DRAW 2

When you play this card, reverse the direction of play AND make the next player in that direction draw 2 cards and lose their turn. This card matches all other Reverse cards and Draw 2 cards and can be played on either type regardless of color.

### REVERSE SKIP

When you play this card, reverse the direction of play AND the next player in that direction is skipped and loses their turn. This card matches all other Reverse cards and Skip cards and can be played on either type regardless of color.

### WILD POWER REVERSE

When you play this card, reverse the direction of play, choose the color that continues play, AND take another turn!

### WILD NO U

You may play this card:

- In response to any Action Card that makes you draw cards. You reverse the direction of play, force the player who played the Action Draw Card to take the penalty, and choose the color that continues play.

- On any card as a normal Wild Card to reverse the direction of play and then choose the color that continues play.

### FOR COLORBLIND PLAYERS

Special graphic symbols have been added to each card to help identify the color(s) on that card. This will allow players with ANY form of color blindness to easily play!
