### Required base game

THESE CARDS ARE DESIGNED TO BE COMBINED WITH A CLASSIC UNO® CARD GAME AND CANNOT BE PLAYED WITH ON THEIR OWN.

### UNO® SWAP PACK IN A NUTSHELL

The cards in this pack involve swapping cards with other players, forcing other players to swap cards, or even swapping your entire hand. But you can’t swap the fact that you still gotta yell “UNO!” when you’re down to your last card!

### SWAP PACK CARDS

### SWAP 1

Play this card on a card of matching color or another Swap 1 card. Then you take a random card from another player’s hand and give them a card from your hand.

### REFRESH HAND

Play this card on a card of matching color or another Refresh Hand card. Place your entire Hand at the bottom of the Discard Pile, and then draw the same number of cards from the Draw Pile to make a new hand.

### WILD FORCE TRADE

When you play this card, choose any two players (include yourself if you want to) and force them to swap hands. Then choose the color that continues play.

### WILD PASS HANDS

When you play this card, all players (including you) must pass their hand to the next player in the current order. After everyone has passed their hand, you choose the color that continues play.

### FOR COLORBLIND PLAYERS

Special graphic symbols have been added to each card to help identify the color(s) on that card. This will allow players with ANY form of color blindness to easily play!
