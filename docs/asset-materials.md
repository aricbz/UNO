# UNO: The Rulez — Final Card Assets

70 unique final PNGs: 61 card faces, 5 coins/markers, and 4 package fronts. The latest matching Mercy and No Mercy coins are included.

## Organization and naming

`Card Assets/<game>/<edition, side, or pack>/<asset type>/<filename>.png`

UNO add-on packs live under `UNO/Add-On Packs`. UNO Flip is divided into Light Side and Dark Side. Show Em No Mercy separates Base Game from Expansion, with Cards, Markers, and Coins in their own folders. Package fronts are kept under each pack’s Packaging folder.

Filenames use lowercase words separated by hyphens: game prefix, pack/side when applicable, card or asset name, and color when applicable. Examples: `uno-reverse-pack-reverse-draw-2-green.png`, `uno-no-mercy-mercy-coin.png`. The asset index lists all final paths and readable names.

## Artwork standard

The original designs remain the authority. Preserve intended typography, symbols, textures, shading, outlines, color relationships, and proportions. Repair actual defects without imposing a flat fill, smoothing, or opacity treatment. Keep the two coins cohesive in rim, rendering, scale, and framing while retaining their distinct face designs.

Card and package canvases are 1024 × 1536. Coins and markers are 1024 × 1024. PNGs use sRGB and retain their alpha channels. Organization and renaming did not re-encode or alter the final image bytes.

Original uploads, alternate generations, references, revision copies, obsolete galleries, process logs, and duplicate archives have been removed from this project. One byte-identical duplicate of the Speed Pack Wild was omitted.

Pack card names were checked against Mattel instructions: [Reverse](https://service.mattel.com/instruction_sheets/JCV56-Reverse.pdf), [Speed](https://service.mattel.com/instruction_sheets/JCV57-Speed.pdf), [Stack](https://service.mattel.com/instruction_sheets/JCV58-Stack.pdf), [Swap](https://service.mattel.com/instruction_sheets/JCV59-Swap.pdf).
